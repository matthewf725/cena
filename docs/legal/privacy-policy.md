### Collection points and acknowledgments

We collect account data when you register or edit your profile; order and menu data when you browse or place an order; analytics events only after you opt in; waitlist data when you submit the waitlist form; staff membership data when a restaurant adds or authorizes a staff member; and request data when you use the privacy request form. At these points, the app or form should link to this Notice at Collection and record the applicable notice/version acknowledgment. Current policy version: **2026-11-13**. The acknowledgment confirms notice was presented; it is not consent to analytics. Analytics consent must be separate, optional, and revocable in settings.

## How we use information

We use personal information to provide and maintain Cena; create accounts; display restaurant meal kits; route orders and pickup instructions; communicate about orders or support; manage restaurant access; secure, troubleshoot, and prevent misuse of the Service; honor privacy choices; and comply with law. We use consented first-party analytics to understand how the app is used and improve it. We do not use analytics information for targeted advertising.

Information comes directly from you (account, profile, waitlist, and privacy request fields); from your use of Cena (menu interactions, orders, pickup status, and consented app analytics); and from a restaurant administrator who invites or manages a staff member. We do not buy personal information or obtain it from data brokers for this Service.

If you ask for recipe or preparation help, the app may send recipe data to OpenAI for that feature. In the launch design this feature receives recipe data only, not your account email, display name, phone number, order history, or restaurant staff membership data. Do not include personal information in free text submitted to the feature. OpenAI processes data under its applicable terms and privacy practices; [confirm the specific service, configuration, retention, and contractual terms before launch].

## When we disclose information

We disclose information as follows:

- **Participating restaurant:** We share the customer information needed to accept and fulfill an order, such as the order details and any contact or pickup information shown for that order. The restaurant uses that information to fulfill and support the order and is responsible for its own handling.
- **Service providers:** We use providers for hosting, database, authentication, communications, security, and first-party analytics. They may process information for Cena under instructions and applicable agreements.
- **OpenAI:** Recipe data only, when you invoke the recipe/preparation feature described above.
- **Legal and safety:** We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, protect rights or safety, investigate fraud or security incidents, or establish or defend legal claims.
- **Business transfer:** Information may transfer as part of a merger, financing, reorganization, or sale, subject to applicable law and appropriate protections.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing. If these practices change, we will update this policy and provide any choices and notices required by law before the change takes effect.

### How to submit a privacy request

Use our [Privacy Request Form]([PRIVACY REQUEST FORM URL]) or email [PRIVACY EMAIL] with the subject “Privacy Request.” You may ask us to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of information associated with your account, or opt out of analytics consent. Tell us your request type and the email associated with your account. We will verify requests by matching the request to the account email and, when reasonably necessary, asking for limited additional account information; we will not ask for your password. An authorized agent may submit a request where applicable law permits, and we may require evidence of authority or direct verification from you.

We will respond within the period required by applicable law. If we cannot fulfill a request, we will explain why and provide any review or appeal process that applies. A request concerning information held by a restaurant should be sent to that restaurant as well. If you contact us to delete an account, we may retain information needed to complete outstanding orders, address safety or security matters, comply with law, or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims, and will delete or de-identify it when that need ends.
